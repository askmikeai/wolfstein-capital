About Us

Getting In Early With the Right Founders

Wolfstein Capital is an angel investment group founded by Michael Friedberg and Gabriel Gomez. We back pre-seed startups at the earliest stages — when the idea is fresh, the team is hungry, and a small bet can make a big difference.

We bring more than capital to the table — we offer hands-on support, strategic thinking, and genuine commitment to helping founders get off the ground.

Our Process

How We Work With Founders

01

Discovery

We evaluate startups based on team strength, market size, technology differentiation, and alignment with our thesis. We move fast and respect founders' time.

02

Due Diligence

A rigorous but founder-friendly process. We dig into unit economics, competitive positioning, and product-market fit indicators to build deep conviction.

03

Partnership

Post-investment, we stay involved. We make introductions, give honest feedback, and help founders navigate the early chaos of building a company.

04

Growth

As portfolio companies grow, we help connect founders with larger investors, advisors, and opportunities to take their startup to the next level.
