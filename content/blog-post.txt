Wolfstein Capital backs founders from day one.

We are an angel investment group founded by Michael Friedberg and Gabriel Gomez, built around a simple thesis: the best time to support an exceptional founder is before the market fully understands what they are building.

At the pre-seed stage, the company is still forming. The idea is fresh, the team is hungry, and a small check can make a real difference. We look for scrappy founders in high-growth markets where speed, judgment, and persistence matter.

Our focus includes artificial intelligence, financial infrastructure, enterprise software, health technology, climate, clean energy, and decentralized systems. But we bet on people first. The right founders will figure out the rest.

Post-investment, we stay close. We make introductions, give direct feedback, help founders sharpen their strategy, and connect growing companies with the next investors, advisors, and opportunities they need.

Wolfstein Capital exists to be an early believer for ambitious builders.
